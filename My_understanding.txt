Cricket Tournament Points Table and Net Run Rate Calculator
This project is a Python-based application developed to manage a 20-over cricket tournament. The system allows tournament organizers to register teams, record match results, calculate points, maintain team statistics, and generate the final standings based on points and Net Run Rate (NRR).
The application accepts a user-defined number of teams and ensures that each team has a unique name. Every team must play against every other team exactly twice during the tournament. Match details such as runs scored, wickets lost, overs faced, and balls played are captured for both teams and validated before being processed.
The program follows standard cricket tournament rules where a winning team receives 2 points, a tied match awards 1 point to each team, and the losing team receives no points. Detailed statistics are maintained for each team, including matches played, wins, losses, ties, points, runs scored, runs conceded, overs faced, and overs bowled.
The Net Run Rate is calculated according to official rules, including special handling for teams that are all out before completing 20 overs. The application performs extensive validation to prevent invalid data from affecting tournament results.
After all matches are completed, the system generates a final points table sorted by total points and Net Run Rate, providing a complete summary of tournament performance for all teams.
